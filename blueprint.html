The verdict: does AI fit Reynolds & Co?

Yes, but not where you think.

You told us the problem is the pipeline. Your own answers describe two estimators pricing the same steel package eight per cent apart, and jobs whose real margin nobody sees until year-end. The margin leaks after you win the work.

01 What We Found
Priority 1 · The same job prices two ways

What's happening. Price the same steel package through two estimators and it lands about eight per cent apart. Nobody can say which was right.

Root cause. Pricing lives in two people's spreadsheets. No single rate card.

What it costs. Roughly £170K a year of margin, given away one quote at a time.

Our recommendation. One rate card first, no AI needed. Then a pricing assistant that works from it.

02 What It Is Worth

The work in this document, priced as the hires it would take: an estimator, a fractional finance director and a commercial assistant. £93,000 to £125,000 a year, before employer costs.
