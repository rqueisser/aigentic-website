Why this matters for Reynolds & Co

Most of Reynolds' work comes through referrals from a few main contractors. Engineers looking for a new fabricator increasingly start by asking an AI assistant, and today it sends them to Meridian and Hallam.

What AI Systems Say

Three questions, written the way a buyer would ask them, put to ChatGPT and Perplexity.

"Best structural steel fabricators in the North of England"
Reynolds & Co: Partial. Absent on Perplexity. Named on ChatGPT in a list of "other regional fabricators", sixth of seven, with no description.
AI named instead: Meridian Structures, Hallam Steel and Northgate Fabrication, each with a sentence on what they do and a link to a project page.
"Alternatives to Meridian Structures for a mid-size steel package"
Reynolds & Co: Absent on both.
AI named instead: Hallam Steel, Castlegate Steelwork and Pennine Structural. Two of the three were cited from a trade directory, one from a trade magazine article.
"What does Reynolds & Co do?"
Reynolds & Co: Present on both. Correctly described as a structural steel fabricator for commercial and civil projects.
Sources: Reynolds' own website, plus a Companies House record. No independent source describes what Reynolds does.

The Gap in Plain English

Three findings explain why other fabricators are named and Reynolds & Co is not.

1
Reynolds is missing from the questions buyers ask. It is absent from both "who should we use" questions on Perplexity, and only just appears on one of them on ChatGPT.
2
AI knows Reynolds only from Reynolds. Every description comes from the company's own website. AI can repeat what Reynolds says about itself, but nothing independent tells it Reynolds is a good answer, so it does not recommend it.
3
The fabricators AI names have what Reynolds lacks. Meridian, Hallam and Castlegate each appear in a trade directory, have project pages that name the region they work in, and have been written about in the trade press in the last year. Reynolds has none of the three.

What Comes Next

This Snapshot tested three questions on two AI tools. The full work agrees the questions Reynolds' buyers really ask, puts every one to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, and turns the results into two documents: a short Brief to decide from, and a Plan to work from. The Plan covers four areas:

Profile

Make sure AI can tell exactly who Reynolds is: the website's company details, directory listings and a clear About page.

Content

One page for each question Reynolds is missing from, written in the buyer's words, so AI has something to quote.

Citations

The trade titles, directories and associations AI already trusts in this sector, and how to get Reynolds mentioned in them.

Tracking

The same questions asked again six and twelve weeks after the changes go live, so the movement is measured, not assumed.
