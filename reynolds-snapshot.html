"Alternatives to the large steel fabricators for civil engineering"

Meridian: Described from trade press, directories and a Wikipedia entry.

The Gap in Plain English

Three findings explain why Meridian appears and Reynolds & Co does not, and why that absence is an opening.

1
AI cannot place Reynolds in the market. There is no structured entry (Wikidata, Wikipedia, a complete Companies profile) that tells a model what Reynolds is and where it fits. Meridian has that scaffolding, so every platform can resolve them cleanly and confidently list them.
2
One platform sees them, the other does not. Reynolds shows up on ChatGPT's "best contractors" list but is invisible to Perplexity, which many engineers and buyers now use. The difference is citations: the only sites linking to Reynolds are Reynolds' own. Meridian is referenced by trade publications and directories, so every AI platform finds them.
3
The specialist question is where it hurts most. When a buyer asks for alternatives, Reynolds does not appear anywhere, despite being exactly the kind of specialist those buyers want. Their genuine strengths only surface when someone already knows to ask for "Reynolds & Co" by name.
